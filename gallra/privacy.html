Gallra Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026

The short version: Gallra collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing outside your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Gallra has no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising, no accounts, and no server. The app contains no networking code. Its App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected.”

Your photos

Gallra reads your photo library through Apple’s PhotoKit framework solely to show you your own photos and carry out the keep, delete, and album actions you choose. Photos are processed on your device and never leave it. Your decisions are stored in a local database on your device and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes that database.

Deletion safety

Photos you delete are moved by iOS to the system’s Recently Deleted album, where iOS keeps them for around 30 days before permanent removal. You can restore them from the Photos app during that time.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see your payment details, name, or Apple Account. Apple’s own privacy policy governs that transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Gallra collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Since the app cannot send or receive data, any change would only ever describe that same fact in new words.
