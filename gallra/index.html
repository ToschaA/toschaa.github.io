How it works

1. Pick a month — or hit Random

Your library is split into small, finishable chunks with visible progress. No “here are 40,000 photos.”

2. Swipe

Left to delete, right to keep, up to file into an album, down to skip for later. Tap any photo to inspect it fullscreen.

3. Review, then one confirmation

Nothing is deleted while you swipe. You see everything you marked, rescue anything with a tap, and confirm once. Deleted photos stay in iOS’s Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Actually private

Gallra’s App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected” — because there is nothing to collect it with. No analytics, no account, no server, no network requests. The only network traffic your purchase ever touches is Apple’s own App Store. Read the whole privacy policy — it fits on one screen.
